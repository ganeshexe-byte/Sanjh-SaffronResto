Our Story

It began in 1985 with a small family kitchen, a single tandoor and one simple promise: feed every guest like family. Back then, our founders served hot chai and homemade tiffin to neighbours who stopped by on their way home in the evening, the hour we call Saanjh.

Word spread, the tables grew, and the menu grew with them. Over the years we added the flavours we fell in love with while travelling, from wood-fired Italian pizzas to Pan-Asian noodles, but our heart stayed in the Indian kitchen, with fresh spices and a pinch of saffron in everything we love most.

Today, Saanjh & Saffron is an all-day restaurant, from the first filter coffee at sunrise to the last sweet bite at night. Every dish is still cooked with the same care, and every guest is still welcomed like family.
